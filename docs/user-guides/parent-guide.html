Chapter 2

Building your child's passport

The passport is the heart of the app — a single, living picture of your child that travels with them between home, school, and any clinicians you connect. It takes about ten minutes to build, and you can always come back and change it later.

Four short sections

We ask about your child in four parts. None of it is compulsory beyond the basics — fill in what feels useful, skip what doesn't.

1. About your child
Diagnoses, school, key people
2. Signals
What "okay" and "hard" look like
3. Communication
How your child talks to the world
4. Support strategies
What helps, before, during and after
Everything auto-saves as you go. Close the app mid-section and pick up right where you left off — nothing is lost.
Not finished yet? Your home screen always shows a "Resume passport creation" card, so you're never hunting for where you left off.

Your finished dossier

Once complete, your child's passport becomes a beautifully organised profile you can open, read, and share at any time — we call it the Digital Dossier.

The top of Alfie's completed passport
Scrolling through shows every section

Editing later

Children change, and so should their passport. Tap "Edit" on any section from the Digital Dossier, and your existing answers are all pre-filled and ready to update.

Before 1pm each day, your home screen invites you to check in with one tap.

Tap "Check in now" to start

Four quick taps and you're done.

1. Sleep
How did they sleep last night?
2. Right now
How settled are they this morning?

Anything unusual happen before school?
4. Anything else
An optional note for the teacher

Chapter 5

The afternoon update

Once school finishes, your child's teacher can send back a short update on how the day actually went — so you're never walking into pick-up blind.

An afternoon update: how settled they were, their energy level, any flags, and a note from the teacher

What the colours mean

The badge at the top of the update uses three simple colours, so you can tell how the day went before reading a single word.

Green — Settled and Regulated. A good day, nothing to flag.
Amber — Unsettled and Anxious. A trickier day; worth a gentle check-in at home.
Red — Highly Dysregulated. A hard day. Your teacher's note will usually explain what happened.

Below the badge, you'll also see an energy level (how tired or wired they seemed) and any flags the teacher noticed — plus a free-text note whenever they've added one.

Chapter 6

Logging incidents

Sometimes things happen — a meltdown at a birthday party, a hard moment at bedtime. Writing it down while it's fresh helps everyone around your child spot patterns over time, at home and at school.

We call this an ABC log, short for Antecedent, Behaviour, Consequence — three simple questions: what happened just before, what your child did, and what happened right after. That's it. No clinical training needed.
Start with when it happened and how intense it felt
Then walk through what happened right before

You can start a log from the "ABC Log" quick action on your home screen, or directly from your child's passport. Every log you save appears in the Incident Timeline on the Digital Dossier, alongside anything logged by a teacher or connected clinician — so everyone sees the same picture.

Chapter 7

Connecting your team

Your child's passport becomes far more useful once it's shared with the people supporting them — but you decide exactly who, and you can change your mind at any time.

Sharing with a school

Open the share icon on your child's Digital Dossier. You'll see your child's own passport code — give this to their class teacher. Separately, enter your school's institution code (get this from the school) to approve the school itself.

Your passport code, plus fields to approve a school and connect a clinician
Until you approve your child's school, a teacher can see only a shortened name — first name and last initial. This is deliberate: full details unlock only once you've actively said yes.
Approving a school by its institution code

Connecting a clinician

If your child works with a psychologist, therapist, or other clinician using The Behaviour Hive, enter their clinician code in the same share screen to connect them.

Manage Access

Scroll down on the Digital Dossier to see exactly who has access — every approved school and every connected clinician, in one list.

Manage Access: every school and clinician currently connected

Revoking access

Changed your mind, or a connection is no longer needed? Tap "Revoke Access" next to any school or clinician, at any time. It takes effect immediately.

A Revoke Access button sits beside every school and clinician
Chapter 8

Help & Support

Most hiccups have a quick fix. Here's where to start, and how to reach us for everything else.

Quick fixes first

The app looks out of date or something isn't loading

Close the app fully — swipe up from the bottom and swipe the app away — then reopen it from the icon.

I'm stuck on a spinning or loading screen

Check your internet connection, then force-close and reopen the app.

I forgot my password

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen and follow the link we email you.

The app is showing a screen I can't get out of

Close and reopen the app. If it keeps happening, note which screen it was and contact us below.

I didn't get my sign-up code email

Check your spam or junk folder first — the sender is info@thebehaviourhive.com.

Common questions

The teacher says my code isn't working.
Double-check they're entering your child's passport code (not your school's institution code) — and that you've also separately approved your school in the share screen. Both steps are needed.
How do I stop a school or clinician seeing the passport?
Open the Digital Dossier, scroll to Manage Access, and tap "Revoke Access" beside them. It's immediate, and fully reversible — you can reconnect later with a fresh code if you choose to.
Can I use the app for more than one child?
Each parent account currently supports one child's passport. If you need a second, get in touch and we'll help.
When you contact us
Please include: which screen you were on, what you tapped, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your phone type. This one detail alone usually helps us fix things in a fraction of the time.

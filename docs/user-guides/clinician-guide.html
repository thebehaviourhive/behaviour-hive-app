Chapter 1

Getting verified

Because you'll be working with sensitive clinical information, verification is a genuine, human-reviewed step — not a formality. Here's exactly what to expect.

Your specialty

After registering and choosing "Clinician" as your role, select your specialty. During this trial, full verification is open to Behavioural Psychologists; other specialties can select their role now and will be notified as their track opens.

Selecting your clinical specialty

Your credentials

Every field here exists for a real reason:

  • Full name — how you'll appear to the families you work with.
  • PSI / CORU / BACB numbers — your professional registration, so we can confirm you're who you say you are.
  • Government ID — a one-time check, never shown on your profile or to anyone else.
  • Two declarations — Garda vetting and professional indemnity cover, both required to work with minors.
The credentials form — every field is used once, for verification only

While you wait

Once submitted, your account sits in a genuinely reviewed queue — a real person checks every submission by hand. You'll see this screen until that's done.

Thanks — your credentials are being reviewed

Once approved, your dashboard unlocks fully, and your own clinician code is generated and ready to share.

Chapter 2

Connecting to families

There's no referral system to navigate — connecting to a case is as simple as sharing a code.

  1. Find your clinician code in the More tab (Chapter 1).
  2. Share it with the parent, however suits you both — email, a printed handout, or read aloud in session.
  3. The parent enters it in their own app to connect you.
  4. The case appears on your caseload immediately — no approval step needed on your side.
The parent stays in control
A parent can revoke your access at any time, for any reason, without needing to tell you why. If a family disappears from your caseload, this is almost always what happened — see Chapter 6 for more.

Review cadence

Set how often a case should be flagged for review — 14, 30, 60, or 90 days — from the More tab. Logging an ABC entry against a case counts as a review, resetting its clock automatically.

Setting your case review cadence

Your activity feed

A single feed spanning every case on your caseload — every ABC log and passport update. It deliberately excludes day-to-day parent/teacher traffic like morning check-ins, so it stays focused on what's clinically relevant.

A case's clinical file

Chapter 5

Clinical logging

Add Log is your entry point for recording anything against a case.

  1. Tap Add Log, then choose the case.
  2. Choose the log type.
  3. For an ABC log, work through Antecedent, Behaviour, Consequence, plus an intensity rating and — uniquely on your track — a perceived function.

Every ABC log you write also appears on the shared Incident Timeline the parent and teacher can see — while a separate, private clinical-notes field stays visible to you alone.

Chapter 6

Help & Support

Most hiccups have a quick fix. Here's where to start, and how to reach us for everything else.

Quick fixes first

The app looks out of date or something isn't loading

Close the app fully — swipe up from the bottom and swipe the app away — then reopen it from the icon.

I'm stuck on a spinning or loading screen

Check your internet connection, then force-close and reopen the app.

I forgot my password

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen and follow the link we email you.

The app is showing a screen I can't get out of

Close and reopen the app. If it keeps happening, note which screen it was and contact us below.

I didn't get my sign-up code email

Check your spam or junk folder first — the sender is info@thebehaviourhive.com.

Common questions

My dashboard is still locked.
You're either mid-review or haven't submitted your credentials yet — check Chapter 1. Reviews are done by a real person, so there's no fixed turnaround, but you can tap "Check status" any time.
A family disappeared from my caseload.
The parent has almost certainly revoked your access — they can do this at any time, for any reason, without notifying you first. Reach out to them directly if you'd like to understand why.
Can I recover a case I was accidentally removed from?
Only the parent can reconnect you, using your clinician code again. There's no override on our side, by design.
When you contact us
Please include: which screen you were on, what you tapped, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your phone type. This one detail alone usually helps us fix things in a fraction of the time.
